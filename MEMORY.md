# Moon City After Abundance Visual Memory

## Source
- PDF: `Moon_City After Abundance.pdf`
- Embedded font family extracted from the PDF objects: `Helvetica Neue`
- Embedded weights/styles found: `UltraLight`, `Thin`, `Light`, `Light Italic`, `Roman`, `Medium`, `Medium Italic`, `Bold`

## Design Traits
- Presentation format is widescreen with a restrained academic layout.
- Slides read as gallery walls: large white fields, generous margins, sparse text blocks, and strong image placement.
- Typography relies on weight changes rather than decorative type choices.
- Titles feel light and airy; supporting labels tighten into medium and bold cuts.
- Visual pacing alternates between near-empty spreads and dense archival or industrial imagery.

## Color Memory
- Base paper white: `#f6f1e9` to `#fcfbf8`
- Graphite ink: `#171717`
- Smoke gray: `#59544d`
- Warm ash/taupe: `#b5aa98`
- Rusted brown: `#8f5f43`
- Oxide tan: `#c7a27b`
- Washed steel / pale cyan accent: `#9fb8c4`
- Deep atmospheric blue from the dome/city imagery: `#2a4f7a`
- Vegetation green appears occasionally, but it is secondary to the industrial neutrals.

## Image Language
- Industrial smog, infrastructure, urban megastructures, archival model photography, and speculative city imagery dominate.
- Several images are monochrome or faded, which makes the occasional blue/cyan graphics feel sharper.
- The deck mixes documentary evidence with speculative visuals; the tone is serious, ecological, and post-industrial.

## UI Translation Notes
- Use a mostly white interface with layered paper tones rather than a dark UI.
- Keep the type stack close to Helvetica Neue with light-to-bold contrast.
- Use subtle lines, thin borders, and restrained gradients.
- Let rust, steel blue, and smoke tones appear as accents around controls and framing.
- Motion should feel tactile and deliberate, not flashy, to match the thesis material.
